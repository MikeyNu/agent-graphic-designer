# Asset generation record

Built-in/native image generation mode was used, with opaque backgrounds. Text was deliberately kept out of both images and typeset in HTML.

## Portrait generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-bleed environment for a fictional coastal walking journal web opening, with editable live text later added in HTML, not in the image. Create one richly atmospheric natural coastal landscape, portrait 2:3 composition. An unmarked narrow pale-sand footpath enters from the bottom center, winds through weathered dune grasses and dark low coastal scrub toward a remote rocky headland at mid-right; open slate-green sea at left, a delicate line of surf, soft mist receding into distance. Eye-level walking viewpoint. Upper 45 percent is luminous pale overcast sky, quiet with very subtle cloud texture, intentionally spacious for dark title typography. Cool muted maritime palette, green-gray sea, tobacco and moss grasses, soft silver sky, natural editorial photographic realism, fine tactile detail in foreground and calm haze in distance. Horizon around the middle. Inviting, intimate, contemplative, never dramatic or tourist-advertisement glossy. No person, buildings, recognizable landmark, signs, letters, words, logos, watermark, UI, borders, or artificial blur. This is an imagined place, not a named real destination.

Output: `assets/coast-original.png`; prepared consumer: `assets/coast-mobile.webp`.

## Wide companion prompt

Create a wide landscape 16:9 companion crop of this same imagined coastal scene for a desktop web chapter cover. Preserve the natural editorial photographic quality, muted slate-green sea, moss and tawny dune grasses, pale sand walking path, rocky headland, soft silver cloudy sky, and quiet lighting. Recompose as a wide 16:9 environment, extending the sea to the left and coast to the right. Keep a gently winding sandy walking path entering at the lower center, ocean on the left and rugged coast extending toward the right distance. Keep the upper 45% of the canvas quiet luminous pale clouded sky, intentionally spacious for live HTML title text. No text, logos, borders, buildings, signs, people or recognizable real destination.

Reference: the generated portrait master above. Output: `assets/coast-wide-original.png`; prepared consumer: `assets/coast-desktop.webp`.
