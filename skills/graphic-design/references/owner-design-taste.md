# Owner's interface taste: direction without a new formula

Use this profile before substantial new interface art direction in this repository. It interprets two sets the owner selected on **30 October 2026**: 40 uploads containing 39 distinct images. Read the [first study](owner-reference-study.md) and [workspace/settings study](owner-workspace-study.md) for concrete observations. The original images are not bundled; reading these notes is not personal visual inspection of those images. The owner also explicitly wants motion designed alongside the UI. The subsequent [nine-clip study](owner-motion-study.md) adds timestamped temporal observations with its own evidence limits.

Treat this as a strong starting preference, not a universal style system. Current instructions, approved identity, audience, task, and supplied project references take priority. Do not force the profile onto an unrelated maintenance edit. The owner's approval establishes aesthetic interest in the collection, not approval of every depicted label, control, metric, or claim. Interpretations below remain revisable through feedback.

## 1. Recognize the range

The collection includes quiet cream financial UI, olive and charcoal property analytics, saturated pet-care dashboards, mint mobile nutrition screens, monochrome creator apps, cinematic product imagery, an orange typographic portfolio, and restrained authentication forms. Do not collapse those into one beige palette, one radius, one font, one density, or one hero arrangement.

The strongest shared preference is for visibly considered composition with character: imagery, type, surfaces, spacing, and small details belong to the same visual world. Some examples are dramatic; others express their character through a subtle contour or a few well-placed colors. Beauty, atmosphere, and delight are sufficient reasons for a successful choice. No functional story needs to be invented to explain every curve.

Use these as possible directions, not presets to assign automatically:

| Direction in the selected set | Character to investigate | Relevant examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cinematic and material | Tonal depth, sculptural product light, large crops, restrained surrounding type | R01, R02, R05 |
| Warm and editorial | Open fields, material colors, fine rules, uneven but balanced emphasis | R03, R04, R07 |
| Playful and tactile | Friendly display type, cropped objects, confident color blocks, rounded interactive surfaces | R08, R09, R10, R12 |
| Graphic and assertive | Type as a shape, strong light/dark masses, expressive portraits, sparse vivid details | R06, R17 |
| Quiet utility with expressive imagery | Conventional forms, careful spacing, unusual photography, shaped boundaries | R13 through R16, R18 through R20 |
| Dense and coordinated | Compact internal hierarchy, persistent object context, different panels within one working surface | R21 through R23, R27, R30 through R32 |
| Editorial learning and luminous depth | Square illustrated sequences or a concentrated atmospheric feature with quiet surrounding work | R25, R26 |
| Precise settings | Aligned labels/controls, deliberate section rhythm, small recurring accents, light or dark surface steps | R33 through R40 |

These families overlap. The table is an interpretation of a small selected set, not a classification of all modern design or evidence that these styles are currently prevalent.

## 2. Learn relationships rather than ingredients

### Compose the image and interface together

Investigate how an image occupies the field before treating it as a rectangular asset slot. R01's product group crosses a panel edge; R02's cup straddles two tonal fields; R05's athlete and headline occupy the same scene; R12's objects extend into the neighboring form surface. Their effect depends on scale, crop, overlap, light, and surrounding space together.

Try a meaningful silhouette, an integrated crop, or a shared background when it suits the work. A subtle intervention can be enough. Do not add overlap as a mandatory originality trick or place an arbitrary object over a standard split hero and call the organizing relationship new. Keep essential information and controls outside the image's obstructing area. Distinguish artwork bounds, transparent image bounds, and actual interactive hit areas during implementation.

### Treat a boundary as a design opportunity

Look at the image panel's concave pocket around the back control in R13 and R14, the rounded form plane in R12, and the undulating white header in R17. A surface can be shaped in relation to its neighbor. This creates more character than applying the same large radius to every box.

Use original contours, optical offsets, or cropping where they strengthen the composition. Maintain a coherent family across related surfaces rather than accumulating unrelated shapes. Design the contour around content expansion and real control space; keep actual controls semantic and independent of decorative masks. If the shape fails on a narrow screen, recompose it before sacrificing labels or targets.

### Let typography be expressive at the right scale

R06 organizes a portfolio with broad display letterforms and tightly fitted word masses. R10 pairs a playful greeting with more conventional data text. R03 lets an offer's numerals become the main visual object. R20 needs almost no display typography because the form itself is the subject.

Give display type room to have a voice while keeping labels, tables, instructions, and form text dependable. Inspect actual words, line endings, counters, weight, and image adjacency. Do not infer a font's name from a screenshot or substitute novelty glyphs into operational text. A type-led design still needs real words and readable information.

### Compose color by area and recurrence

Study the ratio and placement of colors, not just their swatches. The small coral ring in R02 has a different role from R10's broad orange, lilac, and pink panels. R07 repeats lavender and mint in the card, chart, and transaction marks. R09 uses a dark rail and dark chart as visual anchors against a pale workspace.

Allow both restrained and expansive color. Repeat a few relationships across the page to create coherence without making every item identical. Separate brand color, category color, selection, and semantic status. A saturated brand surface is not automatically an alert, and every accent does not need to be a CTA. Preserve accurate encodings and non-color cues where meaning depends on them.

### Vary rhythm within a stable system

R03's equal outer tiles contain different internal compositions: object, statement, miniature image strip, category selector, and large offer. R09 juxtaposes aligned lead rows, portrait cards, a dark chart, and compact integration controls. Their variety comes from what happens inside a coherent field.

Keep shared margins, type roles, and recurring controls, then vary image scale, emphasis, grouping, or density where the content warrants it. Do not require every module to begin with an icon, heading, paragraph, and button. Do not confuse a presentation board showing six components with a production requirement to put all six on one page.

### Balance rich moments with quiet ones

The exuberant portfolio has small orderly captions; the altered portraits sit beside quiet forms; the Finexa dashboard gives its lavender objects an open cream ground. Inspect those contrasts in attention. Richness everywhere can lose its effect; quietness everywhere can erase the chosen character.

Let a surface have a memorable visual moment and enough support around it. There is no fixed number of focal points or mandatory decoration budget. Evaluate the whole at actual size, including a busy state. Keep visual texture or material when it improves the experience, even without a task metaphor.

### Find character in working detail

R21's tiny repeated accents, R27's layered object context, R31's aligned table, and R33 through R38's settings grids extend the preference beyond expressive imagery. Quiet utility can be beautiful through type scale, column starts, line weight, surface steps, and optical spacing. R40 shows that a dense collection, open assistant entry, and profile preview can share one identity without sharing one composition. Do not inflate every operational surface into a presentation dashboard.

### Design its behavior at the same time

The owner wants engaging motion designed with the element, including transitions, reveals, hover, and scrolling. Explore the relationship between a resting composition and its movement before finalizing either. An illustrated cover may react playfully; a selected record may remain recognizable while its details open; an expressive page may unfold through coordinated chapters. Use the [shared motion workflow](interface-motion.md), including reliable controls, interruption, and alternate conditions. Preserve beauty and pleasure as valid intentions. Neither a static-first draft with universal fade-ups nor constant motion everywhere expresses this preference well.

### Extend the taste through time

The [nine-clip motion study](owner-motion-study.md) adds multiple temporal voices: playful follow-through, architectural masks, coordinated progress, typographic collage, quiet travel, kinetic illustration, overlapping surfaces, and photographic continuity. Learn the underlying relationship. A late drawn accent, a recognizable crop, or a stable anchor can matter more than the nominal duration. Coherence does not mean one effect everywhere. The clips include presentation-camera moves and an animated poster; neither proves live interface behavior. Admiring a sequence does not endorse its every component, numerical claim, or sample label.

## 3. Resolve the apparent contradictions

Classify the actual relationship before applying an anti-slop rule:

| Question | Apply this distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Is all left-copy/right-image composition forbidden? | The default restriction concerns the stock promotional hero: generic claim and CTA stack beside a detached mockup. It does not ban authentication forms beside expressive imagery, checkout beside an order/product region, or product details beside selection. R01 and R12 through R16 demonstrate this scope. |
| Does a cup over a seam automatically make a good hero? | No. R02 is a particular composition with a central product, distributed supporting content, tonal division, and selective accent. Preserve the relationship as a lesson, not its exact layout or a loophole based on overlap alone. |
| Are pills, stars, gradients, or abstract 3D inherently slop? | No. R03 uses a star motif, R08 uses rounded selected navigation, R12 uses dimensional forms, and R17 uses a thin gradient outline. The ban on announcement pills above hero titles remains. Judge other uses by their actual visual and interaction roles. |
| Are cards inherently generic? | No. R03's modular variation and R10's colorful dashboard are liked examples. Diagnose monotonous hierarchy, weak grouping, or irrelevant content rather than counting rectangles. |
| Must a distinctive design change familiar navigation? | No. Quiet forms and consistent navigation are part of the collection. Put invention into image-making, proportion, type, rhythm, material, or contour while preserving recognizable operations. |
| Does the preference require every screen to look unusual? | No. R18 and R20 show how a restrained utility screen can belong to a distinctive system. Consistency across equivalent tasks is useful. |
| Does an admired image establish good UX? | It establishes a visual reference. Carry over its appeal while independently resolving data, labels, semantics, responsiveness, and behavior. Do not reject its whole aesthetic because one sample label is wrong. |

Keep the [explicit hero defaults](interface-originality.md#1-honor-the-composition-constraint). A new user-approved direction can supersede them; no extra exception is needed for a pattern that was never within the restriction. Do not broaden this collection into permission to reuse the stock marketing hero for every new project.

## 4. Transfer the taste by surface

- **Marketing and portfolios:** Explore image-led scenes, typographic composition, object scale, cropping, editorial sequencing, and a confident material or color world. Let the actual offering and desired feeling guide the route. Keep a discoverable next step. Do not repeat one arresting opening arrangement for every client.
- **SaaS and dashboards:** Preserve the work's information structure while directing its surfaces. Open fields, illustrated summaries, colorful groups, strong dark anchors, expressive headings, and careful chart texture can all fit. Allocate readable space to the relevant decisions. A truthful dense table can sit comfortably within an exuberant identity.
- **Authentication and checkout:** Keep the operational area calm and reliable while allowing an image, object, contour, or material field to carry personality. A split is permissible. Ensure the form and recovery work without the media, then recompose their relationship on small or short screens. Do not always delete the expressive part on mobile.
- **Mobile apps:** Develop a coherent relationship between canvas, cards, content imagery, navigation, and selected state. A floating bar or shaped header can be attractive. Reserve room for it, keep destinations identifiable, and verify the last item, keyboard, large text, and system insets. Do not reproduce screenshot device frames as app chrome.
- **Creative tools:** Make panel hierarchy and workspace ownership understandable. Soft surfaces and sparse pastel accents can provide a pleasing working environment. Keep actual labels and control states strong enough to read; distinguish settings panels from the work being edited.
- **Settings and profiles:** Direct the alignment of labels, descriptions, fields, and section breaks. Use brand accents, a deliberate shell, or a small identity moment where appropriate. Design unsaved, pending, confirmed, failed, and canceled feedback as part of that visual language; do not let an attractive switch conceal its save contract.

Transfer an abstraction such as “quiet form beside expressive image treatment” or “shared warm surface with color recurring in useful details.” Create new assets, proportions, content relationships, and identity. Copying a source's distinctive face treatment, lettering arrangement, logo, testimonials, or exact contour is not learning its principle.

## 5. Design and inspect with this profile

1. **Choose the relevant evidence.** Use a few applicable examples, including one that challenges a narrow interpretation. Read the image study when raw references are unavailable; label that access honestly.
2. **Name what attracts the eye.** Record a short observation about scale, shape, light, texture, spacing, or rhythm, plus what should survive adaptation. Avoid stopping at “clean,” “premium,” or “modern.” The observation can follow an intuitive experiment.
3. **Make independent roughs.** For substantial new compositions, keep content comparable and vary the organizing relationship. Do not count R13-style photography, R14-style streaks, and R16-style distortion on the same form layout as three structural concepts. They may be useful art-direction variants within one concept.
4. **Inspect at multiple scales.** Judge the whole first, then important image/type boundaries and control details, then actual delivery size. Zoom for inspection without pretending enlargement restores absent detail. Avoid choosing a design solely from an attractive floating-window presentation.
5. **Correct locally.** Separate taste, observed visual defects, factual contradictions, and behavior still to test. Strengthen a pale label, clarify a metric, or move a floating control before discarding the identity. Do not reduce every dashboard to gray or every form to a bare column.
6. **Check independence and variety.** Across different briefs, look for repeated silhouettes, default ornaments, and identical palettes. Across the same product's screens, keep equivalent tasks consistent. Do not manufacture difference merely to pass an originality check.

Judge the final visual experience. Naming the right principles and reproducing this vocabulary cannot establish beauty, accessibility, or human approval. Record what was actually inspected and let the owner's later feedback refine this profile.
