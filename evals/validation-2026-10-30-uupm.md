Date: 30 October 2026. Graphic Design baseline: `4a3fb1e74f0ce9a1fb60d006877bda48e3122623`. Companion Web Motion Director baseline: `6cca5082c966629ae5c33c9851e6c47cc575de14`.

## Scope and research evidence

The owner asked for study of [UI UX Pro Max](https://uupm.cc/) as a negative reference and further improvement of the existing skill. This updates the existing source packages. It does not install a new personal skill, deploy a website, or test the public generator under controlled conditions.

The [study](../skills/graphic-design/references/template-convergence-study.md) documents:

- Visual comparison of all 39 published desktop gallery previews after expanding the gallery.
- Full-page screenshot and DOM section inspection of analytics, education, pet grooming, chatbot, creative agency, legal services, real estate, wedding, travel, DEX swap, sales CRM, and image generation.
- Six displayed prompts: analytics, education, pets, legal, DEX, and image generation. The prompts request landing pages with prescribed content and sometimes surface treatments.
- Two local image-demo interactions: a suggested prompt populated its field; a category selection changed the displayed style choices.
- One computed animation declaration on the agency's service strip: 20 seconds, infinite marquee. This is not a playback/performance or accessibility finding.

Published previews are 1280 by 800. Live full-page captures used a desktop viewport of approximately 1348 CSS px. Some previews were inspected in analysis boards; the remaining gallery rows were inspected directly when asset-download files were unavailable. Missing files were not treated as visual evidence. Full pages were inspected through saved overview images or directly emitted screenshots, with DOM text used for section structure. Raw third-party screenshots are not committed.

The study identifies repeated promotional hierarchy, detached secondary tiles, recurring section recipes, proof-shaped content, and style changes that leave structure similar. It records useful task-specific exceptions and the site's explicit landing-page scope. It does not attribute every decision to a particular model, infer authorship, claim every page is identical, or treat a demo's sample claims as verified business facts.

Mobile, keyboard coverage, assistive technology, reduced motion, all controls, forms, generation, wallet operations, backend persistence, performance, and participant studies were NOT RUN. Computed CSS inspection cannot establish all JavaScript motion, perceived smoothness, or a gallery-wide choreography pattern.

## Changes

The package adds the directly routed comparison study and extends the existing originality procedure with surface identification, product-specific material, section-question mapping, structural concept comparison, and review of the evidence supplied by imagery and credibility blocks. The core, review templates, sourcebook, and README agree on those decisions. Useful consistency, expressive craft, and narrow maintenance scope remain protected.

The companion version 1.2.1 compares temporal structure beneath effect changes, distinguishes product demonstrations from live state, and examines both a principal expressive sequence and repeated ordinary actions. The motion advice is explicitly original synthesis rather than recovered UUPM implementation. Runtime helpers and dependencies are unchanged.

## Forward-exercise setup

They were not given expected answers or the parent's diagnosis and were instructed not to read evaluation definitions, validation records, or other agents' artifacts. The scope was a written handoff, not application implementation. The parent reviewed both complete final responses before recording the results below.

The first exercise supplies three original published gallery previews as unapproved visual exploration inputs and asks for public homepage directions for Tide Kiln ceramics workshops and Tracepath deployment comparison.
