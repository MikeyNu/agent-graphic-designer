Laterlight: creative direction and implementation handoff

This is a written concept proposal for a fictional clothing storefront. The working assortment is relaxed separates and light layers; this is a design assumption, not an established brand fact. Design for shoppers comparing silhouette, fit, and styling. The primary journey is to discover an outfit, inspect a garment, choose its variant and size, and complete checkout with clear confirmation.

The creative idea is **one outfit, two kinds of light**. Laterlight becomes a moment that lingers through paired photographs of the same clothing in warm window light and cool evening shade. The mood should be intimate, observant, and slightly cinematic. Proposed opening copy: “Stay a little longer.” Considered alternatives were a garment construction index and a dense outfit contact sheet. Choose the paired scene for its atmosphere and recognizable visual relationship. These are written hypotheses, not tested visual studies.

Open with compact navigation above two adjoining photographic frames. Match the garment’s scale and hemline across the seam, while changing pose and light. Keep the title, a short outfit caption, and “Shop this look” in one solid band beneath the images. This is the defining composition: clothing first, a small invitation second. Do not add an announcement badge, detached promotional graphic, or overlaid copy competing with fabric. Give “Shop all,” search, and the bag persistent, familiar positions.

Continue with the featured outfit’s individual pieces, then a wider garment collection, then one close fabric study with useful construction or care information. End with sizing, delivery, returns, and contact links. Each section answers a different question: What is the look? What can I buy? What is it like? What happens after purchase? Use borderless product tiles with consistent 4:5 images, names, prices, and named color choices. Keep metadata aligned even when names wrap.

Use deep violet `#29263B` for the opening band and primary actions, chalk `#F7F4EE` for shopping surfaces, and pale lilac `#E5E0EC` for supporting fields. Reserve muted apricot `#D49474` for small decorative details. Use violet text on light surfaces and chalk on violet. Propose Bricolage Grotesque Medium for the wordmark and short headings, with Source Sans 3 for navigation, descriptions, and prices. Begin with 44px desktop headings, 32px mobile headings, 16px body text, and 14px metadata. Verify font files, weights, glyphs, and web licenses before implementation.

Commission or create two coordinated campaign frames, separate mobile crops, neutral front and back product images, and one fabric close-up. Keep garment construction, color identity, and proportions consistent. Campaign lighting may be expressive; neutral product views must explain the item accurately. Preserve faces, hands, and garment edges in crop testing. Record asset provenance and rights. Generated concepts remain labeled internally and cannot substitute for accurate merchandise photography at launch.

Product pages keep image, price, size guide, named swatches, availability, and purchase controls together. Require size selection with an inline explanation. Confirm additions only after cart success; show pending feedback and prevent duplicate submission. Failed additions preserve selections and offer retry. Empty searches retain filters with a clear reset. Bag editing updates totals, and checkout exposes delivery costs and return terms before payment. Preserve browsing filters and scroll position on Back. Product facts, prices, inventory, policies, and payment integration remain required inputs.

On phones, show one campaign portrait followed by its companion, shorten their height, and keep shopping access immediate. Use two product columns when names remain readable. For product galleries, propose a 180ms crossfade when selecting a labeled angle thumbnail, keeping image bounds and purchase controls fixed. Support pointer, keyboard, and touch. Rapid changes cancel obsolete transitions; reduced motion switches directly. Keep text and controls available throughout.

Before release, inspect complete desktop and mobile flows, keyboard focus, image failures, stock changes, payment interruption, contrast, 320px reflow, and enlarged text. No imagery, layout, animation, or commerce behavior has been built or verified in this written handoff.
