# Asset production record

Use one short row or section per distinct asset. Omit fields that do not affect the job. Keep private prompts, source links, and generation receipts outside public runtime assets.

- **Role and consumer:** Surface, section/component, and visual contribution.
- **Source and medium:** Approved/reused, generated, edited, licensed, or exact construction; source/reference and license.
- **Composition contract:** Subject, ratio, focal area, alpha/opaque choice, palette/lighting, text-safe area, allowed crop, smallest use, and invariants.
- **Family:** Anchor and properties shared with siblings; icon family when relevant.
- **Actual output:** File/reference, native dimensions, format, hash, derivatives and transformations. Leave unknown fields unknown.
- **Destination and consumer:** Verified project file and actual import/URL; transfer/hash check when crossing machines.
- **State:** Planned, generated, prepared, installed, integrated, verified, or blocked with reason.
- **Inspection:** Whole image, native details, light/dark/actual mattes when relevant, and integrated sizes/states. Record method and evidence; unperformed checks stay NOT RUN.
- **Repair queue:** Observed region/placement, severity, chosen operation, preserved invariants, result and recheck.
- **Conclusion:** Passes visual QA for the stated use, needs revision, or not assessed; remaining limits. Generated origin stays recorded.
