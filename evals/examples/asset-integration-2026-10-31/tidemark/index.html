THE EDGE OF QUIET

Where the path
grows quiet.

A SAMPLE PASSAGE / 2 MIN READ

IN THE MARGIN

Leave room
for getting
nowhere.

At first, there was only the path: a pale seam between the grass and the sea. It narrowed as the houses in my mind fell away, until there was barely room for anything but one foot in front of the other.

The tide had left a dark line on the rocks. Below it, little pools held pieces of the sky. I stopped at one and watched a cloud cross the water without disturbing it.

Further on, the grass leaned into the wind. There was no sign telling me what this place was called, no bench to suggest the best view. Just a curve in the coast, and another beyond it.

I had meant to walk until I found something worth writing down. Instead, I sat on a warm stone and let the page stay empty for a while.

The sea went on with its work.
