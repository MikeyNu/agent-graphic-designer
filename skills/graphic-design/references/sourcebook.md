# Research sourcebook

Research reviewed on 18 October 2026. Preserve these distinctions when using or updating the skill:

- **Research finding:** A result within a particular study's sample, task, stimulus, and method.
- **Technical requirement:** A specification within its stated medium and scope.
- **Practitioner account:** A useful description of a designer's method or intended effect.
- **Design synthesis:** The workflow, tests, examples, and critique rubric proposed by this repository.

Do not turn any of them into a guarantee of taste, originality, human response, accessibility, or commercial success. Most procedural instructions in the skill are design synthesis. Source notes identify the narrower claims supported by published material.

## Refresh procedure

Before a substantial claim about current design practice, inspect dated independent applications and their actual images. Record category, region, audience, original project date, observed device, rationale, and limits. Keep a historical reference when it clarifies how a device is being reinterpreted.

Recheck changing technical documentation, platform requirements, font/asset availability, and license terms when applying them. Verify arithmetic and actual output behavior even when a source is official. Distinguish a source becoming unavailable from its claim becoming false; use an original publisher, institutional record, or author copy where available and state the access limit.

Update a rule because new evidence changes the relevant conclusion, not because a trend list announces a new year. Preserve the distinction between a study result, an inference from a case, and the agent's design judgment.
