# Creative responsibility and open-brief validation

31 October 2026. The owner clarified the skill's purpose: bridge incomplete prompts and intentional visual work by supplying creative judgment across interfaces, graphics, identities, brand guidelines, and motion. Polished but interchangeable output remains a failure of specificity. Sparse and rich compositions can both succeed.

## Change and research scope

The core now makes completing the creative brief an explicit responsibility. The new [creative-direction guide](../skills/graphic-design/references/creative-direction.md) separates reversible aesthetic decisions from factual unknowns, starts from the particular subject, uses research to widen possibilities, develops image/type/color/layout/motion together, and includes useful unrequested finishing decisions. It also describes how an identity becomes a usable brand guide with applications and annotations. Existing asset-generation and execution guidance remain in place.

The [sourcebook](../skills/graphic-design/references/sourcebook.md#creative-direction-and-open-briefs) records primary research and method sources, including contrasting findings on AI and diversity. Neither model homogeneity nor human freedom from fixation is treated as a universal fact. The workflow is design synthesis, not a scientifically validated creativity guarantee.

## Fresh-context written exercises

Two independent agents received only the skill package location, the task below, a task-local output destination, and instructions to use plain language without em dashes. They were told not to inspect repository evaluation examples or sibling task folders. They did not receive the owner's explanation, evaluator diagnoses, desired answers, or each other's output. No corrective feedback was given before these outputs were retained.

Task portion supplied for Laterlight:

> I want a beautiful clothing storefront for a fictional business named Laterlight. Prepare the written creative direction and implementation handoff first, within about 650 words. This request is for the written handoff only.

Task portion supplied for Brass Window:

> Create the art direction and production plan for a distinctive brand-guidelines document for Brass Window, a fictional VR company. No identity assets have been supplied. For now deliver a written handoff within about 650 words, not the finished document.

| Retained output | Observed behavior | Limits and concerns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Laterlight handoff](examples/creative-direction-2026-10-31/laterlight.md), 635 words | Developed paired clothing scenes under different light, a specific image/caption arrangement, palette and type proposals, asset requirements, responsive behavior, and a restrained gallery transition. Labeled the assumed assortment and withheld product/policy claims. | No visible concepts, generated assets, rendered typography, or executed flow. The preferred direction remains a proposal. Operational icon selection is not resolved in this concise handoff. |
| [Brass Window handoff](examples/creative-direction-2026-10-31/brass-window.md), 635 words | Developed a viewpoint-change concept with three proposed routes, image-production method, identity considerations, page sequence, annotation space, application examples, motion storyboard, deliverable inventory, and inspection plan. Kept the identity provisional. | No rendered guide, identity assets, scene continuity check, typography comparison, or motion playback. The proposed wordmark and palette require visual testing. |

Both agents made creative choices without returning a design questionnaire or inventing a verified business history. Both stayed within the written-handoff scope and disclosed unperformed production checks. These are observations of planning transfer, not ratings of finished beauty or proof that every sparse brief will succeed. Both names offered visual associations; transfer to an abstract name, a dense operational surface, and deliberately exuberant work remains a useful future test. No universal palette or chosen composition should be inferred from these examples.
