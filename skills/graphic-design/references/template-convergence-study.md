It informs the [originality workflow](interface-originality.md); it is not an authorship detector, a controlled comparison of generators, or a prohibition on the site's ingredients.

## 1. Evidence and limits

The live gallery displayed 39 demos. All 39 published desktop previews were visually compared after expanding the gallery. Twelve destinations received full-page screenshot inspection and DOM heading/section inspection. Six displayed prompts were read: analytics, education, pet grooming, legal services, DEX swap, and image generation. The table below separates these access levels.

Full-page inspection means composition, section sequence, and selected detail, not an exhaustive actual-size audit of every pixel. Gallery previews were published 1280 by 800 images; live full-page captures used a roughly 1348 CSS px desktop viewport. Here, floating tiles describe detached visual placement, not verified movement. The study did not test mobile layouts, keyboard coverage, screen readers, reduced motion, network failure, performance, all buttons, or backend completion. A gallery image may differ from the current live page.

Two local controls on the image-generation demo were exercised: choosing a suggested prompt populated the input; choosing a different style category changed the displayed style options. Generation was not submitted. On the creative-agency page, the computed style of the service strip declared a 20-second infinite marquee. That declaration does not establish perceived smoothness, pause behavior, reduced-motion support, or all JavaScript-driven motion. No gallery-wide animation audit was performed.

This is one site's curated collection, not 39 independent studies. The demos are presented as examples. Their sample testimonials, counters, and product claims are not verified business evidence and are not allegations of fraud. The public generator was not run under controlled prompts; neither its current implementation nor its responsibility for any individual decision was established. Later readers of these notes must not claim personal viewing of unavailable images.

## 3. What repeats and what differs

The strongest recurring resemblance is the relationship between blocks, not one color, typeface, border radius, or component library. These are original visual judgments grounded in the inspected examples.

| Observed recurrence | Concrete instances | Design implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A small promotional label precedes the headline; an accent emphasizes a few headline words; supporting copy leads to actions and proof. | Analytics, education, pets, travel, health, banking, AI tools. | Restyling each slot can preserve the same hierarchy. Inspect the whole arrangement before calling it a new concept. |
| A detached product/image panel carries smaller floating tiles around it. | Analytics statuses, pet services, wellness progress, coworking amenities, medical credentials. | The tiles often occupy the same compositional role across unrelated domains. Ask whether this relationship belongs to the particular content. |
| Material changes exceed structural changes. | Soft pet surfaces, outlined education cards, dark financial panels. | A style label and palette can change the finish while leaving the underlying proposal largely unchanged. |
| Similar section headers lead into equal feature, service, testimonial, or price cells. | Analytics, chatbot, pets, education, travel, wedding. | Check whether each section has an independent question and whether its form expresses that relationship. Equal cards remain suitable for genuinely comparable items. |
| Generic uplift and credibility occupy substantial space. | Repeated broad promises, large counts, ratings, endorsements, and closing invitation blocks. | Replace filler with approved specifics. In a real deliverable, source the evidence or omit the unsupported block. |
| More emphatic styling does not ensure a more specific idea. | The agency's bright planes and huge type still accompany a broad promotional claim. | Brutalism, serif editorial styling, handmade texture, or unusual motion can become another template. Judge execution and fit. |

There are meaningful differences. Property and travel pages expose search; adoption previews expose animals; DEX shows a swap form; image generation places prompt entry near the top. The agency's ruled service rows differ from the repeated card grid. The art-gallery preview gives imagery a broad horizontal field. These exceptions prevent an honest study from saying every page is identical or every familiar control is wrong.

Repeated navigation and matching behavior within one product are useful consistency. Repetition becomes a design concern when unrelated offerings inherit the same unexamined hierarchy, content allocation, and motion regardless of their needs. Familiarity alone is not a defect.

## 4. Twelve full-page observations

These short notes add whole-page context to the opening previews. They are critique of demo compositions, not usability-study results.

| Page | Observation and lesson |
| --- | --- |
| Analytics | The split opening leads to six feature cells, another dashboard preview, three price tiers, trust material, a closing invitation, and a large footer. The chart vocabulary supplies category recognition, but sample metrics and a chart picture are not proof of analytical usefulness. Preserve actual definitions and decisions when building analytics. |
| Education | Strong outlines and displaced shadows give the interface a different material character. Course rows, four benefits, three testimonials, and a closing trial panel still create a familiar sequence. Keep the useful course metadata; derive learning structure from what someone must choose or resume. |
| Pet grooming | Warm rounded surfaces unify service, animal, pricing, review, and booking sections. Their repeated headers and box treatments flatten distinct kinds of information into a similar rhythm. Preserve warmth while separating service choice, suitability, and booking. Check picture/caption correspondence as carefully as styling. |
| Chatbot | Centered introduction, feature cells, an example conversation, integrations/code, price tiers, and a closing CTA show that centering the hero does not remove template repetition. A concrete example can explain the product; a generic conversation alone cannot establish all claimed capabilities. |
| Creative agency | Primary colors, hard shadows, oversized type, a service marquee, project tiles, ruled service rows, team cells, and a contact form produce a stronger tonal change. In the captured desktop opening, the large colored planes obscure part of the final headline word. Repair that specific overlap without declaring expressive typography or asymmetry unacceptable. |
| Legal | Image-backed opening, practice-area cells, attorney portraits, result figures, testimonials, awards, and consultation form build a familiar trust narrative. The dark headline is difficult to distinguish in the captured image field; no numeric contrast audit was performed. Credentials and results require verification in a real client site. |
| Property | The opening photograph, search controls, listing metadata, agents, and enquiry form make the category legible. Listing cards are a reasonable comparison unit. Retain the task-specific structure while checking search, details, and enquiry separately; their appearance does not demonstrate working integrations. |
| Wedding | The image field and varied photo mosaic supply atmosphere, while service cards, vendor cells, planning-tool cards, testimonials, metrics, and the enquiry form extend a long sequence. Keep photographic appeal. Edit repeated persuasion around the actual planning stage instead of imposing compulsory minimalism. |
| Travel | Search and destination metadata provide concrete browsing affordances. The photo collage, detached trip tile, benefits, reviews, and closing CTA also echo other demos. Use actual itinerary and comparison needs to choose content priority; a pleasing collage cannot substitute for accurate destination imagery. |

## 5. What the displayed prompts explain

The six inspected prompts all requested landing pages and prescribed categories of content. Several also named a surface style, color mood, or genre. Analytics requested glass-like panels and trust material; education and pets requested playful dimensional treatments; legal specified a conventional professional palette; DEX specified a swap preview; image generation specified previews, styles, and credit pricing.

This helps explain the brief's contribution to repeated ingredients. It does not establish the generator's internal rules or prove causation. A marketing page is an appropriate response to a marketing-page brief. Do not criticize these examples merely for lacking an entire authenticated application, and do not give a marketing page to a user who requested an operational workspace.

The homepage illustrates database lookup for product, style, type, color, and landing patterns. Such resources can support a designer. The failure mode to guard against is allowing category lookup to settle the composition before the particular product, evidence, and content relationships have been understood.

The illustrated lookup also includes loading-only animation advice. That is one displayed example, not an audited policy of the generator. Our motion direction considers feedback, continuity, narrative, and expressive pleasure as well as loading; a category-level rule should not decide every sequence.

## 6. Transfer into design and motion decisions

Use the [composition procedure](interface-originality.md#3-derive-alternatives-from-the-product) and its product-specific section map. Keep the following distinctions visible during review:

1. **Vocabulary versus concept.** Glass, dark mode, serif type, soft shadows, and spring motion describe treatments. They do not by themselves define a visual idea, information architecture, or behavior.
2. **Domain nouns versus domain structure.** Replacing a dog with a doctor in the same panel changes subject matter. A design becomes more specific when service suitability, availability, comparison, or another actual relationship changes the hierarchy.
3. **Proof versus proof-shaped decoration.** A review panel, chart, counter, customer portrait, or status toast must contain appropriate evidence or explicitly identified fixtures. Leave unsupported proof out of real releases.
4. **Section rhythm versus container repetition.** Give different questions suitable structures. Do not add arbitrary layout changes merely to pass a variety quota, or discard comparable cards and reliable navigation.
5. **Motion language versus an effect preset.** Plan which object leads, what stays still, what changes, and how movement ends. Identical fade-ups, perpetual floating tiles, and repeated count-ups are review triggers in our output, not findings established across every UUPM page.
6. **Demonstration versus live state.** Label a simulated chart, typing sequence, generation preview, or transaction as a demonstration. Animation completion cannot manufacture a successful operation or a real metric.
7. **Expressive craft versus an anti-template costume.** Keep beautiful atmosphere and useful conventions. Resolve actual defects locally, then test a different organizing relationship when the concept itself is interchangeable.

The owner's explicit hero defaults remain in force. This study adds evidence and a repair method; it does not broaden those defaults into bans on all splits, cards, gradients, ornament, or motion. It also does not make every future design a property search, an editorial page, or a silent utilitarian interface.
